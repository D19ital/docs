Sculpt is a set of sculpted keycaps for ergonomic split keyboards. The goal is not decoration, but matching the shape of the keycap to the movement of the hand

## Why it exists

On a flat keyboard, almost any profile can be made to work. On a split keyboard, that is no longer enough: the halves are separated, the columns are staggered, and the thumb cluster lives in its own geometry. Sculpt reduces the conflict between the case, the layout, and finger motion

## Geometry

Each key in Sculpt has its own height, angle, and tilt. This is not just different rows — it is a multi-axis geometry that helps the finger land more naturally

The concave top makes the key easier to read by touch, not only by sight. On an ergonomic keyboard, that matters because people often rely on muscle memory and tactile navigation

## Thumb cluster

The thumb moves differently from the other fingers. That is why Sculpt gives the thumb cluster its own shape, angle, and sweep

## Tactile cues

Sculpt uses a blank design with no legends on the top surface. Navigation comes from index keys, surface texture, and internal part marking for assembly and QC

## Manufacturing

Sculpt is printed with SLA using ABS-Like resin. That choice is driven by the geometry itself: the profile needs a process that can reproduce small transitions, local tilts, and fine surface detail without heavy compromises
