Sculpt — sculptural keycaps for ergonomic split keyboards

![Sculpt_example](/images/sculpt/scupt_phenom.jpeg)

## Installation

Sculpt comes as a set of keycaps for self-assembly on your keyboard. All keycaps are marked with index labels on the keycap itself in the form "XY", where X is the letter of the row (top to bottom), Y is the sequential number of the column from left to right

![Sculpt](/images/sculpt/cap_index.png)

{{< callout type="warning" >}} Correct keycap installation — the edge with the index label must be on top {{< /callout >}}

## Troubleshooting

{{< details title="Keycap does not match the geometry or height" closed="true" >}}

- Check the switch installation, the switch must be fully inserted
- Check that the keycap index matches its correct placement on the keyboard
- Check the keycap stem for foreign objects, such as support remnants. Clean the stem with a thin object

![Sculpt](/images/sculpt/cap_clear.png)

{{< /details >}}

## Care and maintenance

{{% steps %}}

### Remove the keycaps

Use a special puller to avoid scratching the sides

### Make a solution

Pour warm water (up to 40°C) into a container and add a couple drops of soap or dishwashing liquid

{{< callout type="warning" >}} Do not use boiling water — high temperature will deform the keycap stem, and it will no longer hold on the switch {{< /callout >}}

### Soak

Leave the keycaps in water for 10–15 minutes

### Wipe

Use a soft toothbrush or microfiber without applying strong pressure

### Rinse

Rinse the keycaps in clean running water

### Dry

Place the keycaps stem-down on a towel until completely dry

{{% /steps %}}

{{< callout type="error" >}} Do not use aggressive cleaning agents (acetone, gasoline, solvents (646, white spirit), concentrated acids and alkalis)! {{< /callout >}}
