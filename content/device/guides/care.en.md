---
title: Care and maintenance
weight: 3
---

- Periodically clean the case using a dry or slightly damp cloth
- Disconnect the cable and turn off the device before cleaning
- Do not allow dirt to accumulate around switches, buttons, connectors, or moving parts

{{< callout type="error" >}}
Do not use aggressive cleaning agents such as acetone, gasoline, solvents, concentrated acids, or alkalis
{{< /callout >}}

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
Do not allow liquid to get inside the device
{{< /callout >}}

See [K:04 Modules](/device/k04/modules/#trackball-care) for trackball ball and bearing maintenance
