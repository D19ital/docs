Ergohaven devices come with a 3-year warranty and service support

If you have questions about configuration, firmware, or operation, use the [knowledge base](https://docs.eh.industries/) or contact the [Ergohaven Telegram group](https://t.me/c/1464748383/8323)

When requesting support, include the device model, firmware version, operating system, and connection method
