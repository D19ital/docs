Wireless RMK keyboards work with Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS using Plug and Play. No additional drivers are required

Fully charge the device and make sure its power switches are on before first use

## USB connection

Connect a data-capable USB-C cable to the keyboard's central half and the computer

{{< callout type="info" >}}
The left half is the **central** half on the K:04 Series
{{< /callout >}}

## First Bluetooth connection

1. Turn on both halves — they are synchronized at the factory
2. Select a free BT profile on the keyboard if necessary
3. Enable Bluetooth on the computer, tablet, or phone
4. Find the keyboard in the list of available devices and pair it

You can skip profile selection for the first connection: the keyboard uses the current profile

## Connecting an additional device

1. Enable Bluetooth on the new device
2. Select another BT profile on the keyboard
3. Wait for the cyan indicator to blink, showing that the keyboard is ready to pair
4. Find the keyboard in the Bluetooth device list and connect it

The standard layout on your model page shows the BT profile keys. On K:04, they are on layer 3

See [K:04 LED indication](/device/k04/indication/) for connection and pairing signals
