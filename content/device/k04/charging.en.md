---
title: Charging and battery monitoring
weight: 3
---

- Use the included USB-C cable or another USB-C cable with a computer USB-A port or a 5 V charger
- A full charge takes approximately 1.5–2 hours for each half
- Without Qube, check each half using [LED indication](../indication/) and the **BT Battery** key; with Qube, check the level on its display
- You can continue using the keyboard while it is charging
- Fast charging is not supported

{{< callout type="important" >}}
To charge a half, its power switch on the bottom must be in the **ON** position
{{< /callout >}}

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Charge both halves for 1.5–2 hours before using the keyboard for the first time
{{< /callout >}}
