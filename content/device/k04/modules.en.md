## Trackball care

Keep the ball, ceramic bearings, and ball socket clean

{{< callout type="info" >}}
To remove the ball, pull it out in the direction opposite the retaining tab on the module case
{{< /callout >}}

See [Care and maintenance](/device/guides/care/) for general recommendations
